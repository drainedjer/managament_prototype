Terms of Service

Clear rules governing applications, bed reservations, lease terms, and use of the CasaBatangueño platform.

1. Applications & Bed Reservations

Submitting an online application does not immediately create a tenancy. Once approved or reserved with an assigned room, a bed is held for 7 calendar days (Asia/Manila time). Unconfirmed reservations expire automatically so beds remain available to other applicants.

2. Rent, Utilities & Official Receipts

Monthly rent and applicable sub-metered utility bills are due on the date specified in your lease agreement. Partial or full installment payments are recorded in our ledger and reflected in your Account Portal with official receipt numbers.

3. Security Deposits & Move-Out

Security deposits are held against unpaid balances, final utility meter readings, and damage beyond normal wear and tear, and are settled upon completion of the move-out clearance checklist.

4. Acceptable Use

Applicants and residents agree to provide accurate contact information and abide by property house rules, quiet hours, and visitor policies posted on each listing.
