About CasaBatangueño & FAQ

Locally managed student dormitories, apartments, and private rooms built around safety, quiet study spaces, and transparent policies.

Who Runs CasaBatangueño

CasaBatangueño is owned and directly operated by Lance Caringal together with our on-site administration and caretaker team in Brgy. Alangilan, Batangas City. Every property is inspected weekly, maintained by our in-house staff, and supported with digital billing and official receipts.

Our Mission

To give students, board-exam reviewees, and young professionals clean, reliable, and fairly priced housing—with clear terms upfront and responsive local care.

Why Alangilan, Batangas City?

Brgy. Alangilan is one of Batangas City's most convenient residential corridors—home to Golden Country Homes, Villa Josefa, and Jose P. Laurel National Highway. Residents enjoy walkable access to Alfamart, study cafés, affordable eateries, banks, and direct jeepney routes across the city.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you should know before reserving a bed or unit.

Open Find a Rental, click View details on a property, and choose Apply for this property. Submit your name, email, contact number, and target move-in date to receive an instant reference number (APP-YYYY-XXXX).
After your application is reviewed and assigned a room, a reservation fee (₱1,000–₱3,500 depending on the property) holds your slot for up to 7 days. On move-in, 100% of your reservation fee is deducted from your move-in payment.
Your security deposit is fully refundable within 7–14 days after your official move-out date, subject to a room clearance check and settlement of final electricity/water sub-meter readings and any property damages beyond normal wear and tear.
We accept Cash (at our Alangilan office), GCash, Maya, and Bank Transfer. All verified payments receive an official receipt number recorded in our management portal.
Bring 1 valid Government ID, Student ID, or Company ID, an active emergency contact, your signed lease/house rules form, and your move-in payment (1 month advance + security deposit less any reservation fee already paid).
Policies vary by property type: apartments and studios have 24/7 keycard access with registered daytime/evening guests until 10:00 PM, while student dormitories log late check-ins after 11:30 PM and welcome visitors in the ground-floor study lounge from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
High-speed fiber Wi-Fi is included across all properties, and water is included in dormitories and boarding houses. Room/unit electricity (and water in private apartments/studios) is sub-metered per unit or shared fairly among room occupants.
You may cancel a pending application anytime before paying a reservation fee at no cost. Paid reservation fees are non-refundable if cancelled within 48 hours of target move-in, and active tenants are asked to give at least 30 days' written notice before moving out.
