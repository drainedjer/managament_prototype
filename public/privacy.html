Privacy Policy (RA 10173)

Commitment to the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 for applicants, residents, and inquiries in Alangilan, Batangas City.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account, apply for a room or unit, book a viewing, join a waitlist, or send an inquiry, we collect only the details necessary to process housing services: your name, email address, mobile number, target move-in date, optional ID verification document, and—upon contract signing—your home address, emergency contact, and security deposit record.

2. Purpose & Lawful Processing

Your personal data is used strictly to evaluate rental applications, manage bed reservations, administer lease contracts and official payment receipts, coordinate maintenance requests, and respond to inquiries.

3. Role-Based Access & Safeguards

  • Public listings and API endpoints never expose personal contact details, addresses, emergency contacts, or uploaded ID documents.
  • Staff accounts view masked email and phone numbers by default; any authorized reveal or ID document access is permanently recorded in our security audit log.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed with bcrypt, sessions use httpOnly cookies with CSRF protection, and uploaded applicant ID documents are stored privately outside public storage.

4. Your Rights Under RA 10173

As a data subject, you have the right to access, export, correct, and request deletion of your personal data. Customer accounts can download a complete JSON export or delete their account anytime in My Account → Profile & Privacy, or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at our Contact page.
