The 6-Step Move-In Flow

Apply → Review → Reserve → Contract & Deposit → Move In → Monthly Payment & Receipts

1

Apply Online

Choose a property on Find a Rental, submit your contact details and target move-in date, and get an instant reference number (APP-YYYY-XXXX).

2

Owner Review

Our Alangilan management team verifies bed availability within 24 hours and assigns your room or confirms an ocular visit.

3

Reserve Your Bed

Once approved, your bed is held for 7 days. Settle the reservation fee (credited 100% toward your move-in payment).

4

Contract & Deposit

Sign the lease agreement and settle 1 month advance rent plus refundable security deposit prior to move-in.

5

Move In

Complete the room inventory and meter reading checklist with our caretaker and receive your keys.

6

Monthly Billing

Pay rent in full or installments via Cash, GCash, Maya, or Bank Transfer and download official receipts.

Move-In Requirements

Prepare these documents prior to contract signing so your check-in takes less than 15 minutes:

  • Valid Government ID, Student ID, or Company ID (you may optionally upload a private copy when applying).
  • Signed Lease / Dormitory House Rules Agreement (parent/guardian co-signature for minors).
  • Emergency Contact Information (name, relationship, and active mobile number).
  • Move-in Settlement: 1 month advance rent + security deposit (less paid reservation fee).
