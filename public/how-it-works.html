How Renting Works

A clear, predictable 6-step journey from your first online application to monthly receipts.

The 6-Step Move-In Flow

Apply → Review → Reserve → Contract & Deposit → Move In → Monthly Payment & Receipts

1

Apply Online

Choose a property on Find a Rental, submit your contact details and target move-in date, and get an instant reference number (APP-YYYY-XXXX).

2

Owner Review

Our Alangilan management team verifies room/bed availability within 24 hours and matches you with an open unit or schedules an ocular visit.

3

Reserve Your Room

Once approved, settle the reservation fee (₱1,000–₱3,500 depending on unit type) to lock your room for up to 7 days. Reservation fees are credited toward move-in.

4

Contract & Deposit

Review and sign the lease agreement, then settle 1 month advance rent plus 1–2 months refundable security deposit before move-in day.

5

Move In

Complete the room inventory checklist with our caretaker, receive your keys/keycard, and connect to building high-speed fiber Wi-Fi.

6

Monthly Payment & Receipts

Pay monthly rent and sub-metered utility bills on or before the 5th via Cash, GCash, Maya, or Bank Transfer and receive an official digital receipt.

Move-In Requirements

Prepare these documents prior to contract signing so your move-in takes less than 15 minutes:

  • Valid Government ID, Student ID, or Company ID (plus 1 photocopy for building security).
  • Signed Lease / Dormitory House Rules Agreement (parent/guardian co-signature for minors).
  • Emergency Contact Information (name, relationship, and active mobile number).
  • Move-in Settlement: 1 month advance rent + security deposit (less paid reservation fee).

Reservation & Payment Channels

We record every payment in our portal and issue a printable official receipt (RCPT-YYYY-XXXX).

Cash (On-site Office) GCash Maya Bank Transfer

Always include your application reference number or tenant name on transfer notes and keep your screenshot until confirmed.
